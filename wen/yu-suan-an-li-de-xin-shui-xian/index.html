预算案里的薪水线

The Wage Line in the Budget

逆流 · 2026年10月9日

一张预算案，把毕业后背着PTPTN的人，按薪水划出几条还款线。安华10月9日在国会提呈2027年预算案时说，月薪不到2500令吉的借贷者，2027年无需还款；月薪介于2500至3000令吉，每月还50令吉，超过40万名借贷者受惠。这条线很清楚。

高教部截至2026年8月31日的数据是，116万名借贷者拖欠PTPTN共107亿令吉。暂缓还款，对应还不起的人；定期还款、没有拖欠者获一成折扣，对应还得起的人；雇主代员工还款可获税务扣除，《星报》报道适用于2027年1月1日至2028年12月31日的还款，对应有雇主愿意代付的人。三种处境，三本账。

如果一份不到2500令吉的薪水要跨过门槛，才轮到每月还50令吉，那么2027年这一年里，什么会让它变成3000令吉？还款可以缓一年，薪水不会自己长。家里算账时，能把一笔支出往后挪；收入要跨过那条线，又靠什么？

The Wage Line in the Budget

One Budget has drawn several repayment lines, by salary, for people who graduated carrying PTPTN loans. Tabling Budget 2027 in Parliament on 9 October, Anwar said borrowers earning less than RM2,500 a month need not repay in 2027; those earning between RM2,500 and RM3,000 repay RM50 a month; more than 400,000 borrowers benefit. The line is very clear.

The Higher Education Ministry's figures as of 31 August 2026 show 1.16 million borrowers in default on a total of RM10.7 billion in PTPTN loans. Deferred repayment is for those who cannot pay; a 10 per cent discount for regular repayers with no arrears is for those who can; tax deductions for employers who repay on behalf of employees, which The Star reports apply to repayments from 1 January 2027 to 31 December 2028, are for those whose employers are willing to pay. Three situations, three sets of books.

If a salary below RM2,500 must cross the threshold before it is asked to pay RM50 a month, then what, during 2027, would turn it into RM3,000? Repayment can be put off for a year; pay does not grow by itself. At home, when we do the sums, we can push one expense back; for income to cross that line, what does it rely on?
