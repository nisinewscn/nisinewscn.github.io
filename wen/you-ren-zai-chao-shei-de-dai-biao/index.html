有人在朝，谁的代表

Someone's in Government. Whose Representative Is That?

逆流 · 2026年8月7日

华人要有代表，就得投行动党。这句话，我们这一代说了快二十年。七月三十日，森美兰州选投票前两天，华总会长拿督林家全说得更直白：华裔同胞要踊跃投票，是为了确保选举后华社「有人在朝好办事」。

八月一日揭晓的成绩，答案落在了这句话最有资格代言的那个人身上。真纳，行动党秘书长陆兆福从二〇一三年守到二〇二六年的议席，这一次以五千零三十八票，不敌马华萧康骏的五千七百二十六票，输了六百八十八票。成绩由选举委员会公布，官方成绩网站可查。真纳登记选民一万四千四百二十二人，投票率百分之七十五点七一。

三年，同一个人，同一个议席，多数票从两千二百倒退成负六百八十八。

如果「有人在朝」能办成事，那先看「朝」里发生过什么。二〇二二年十一月大选，行动党赢得四十个国会议席，是希望联盟内最大赢家。翻开同年十二月公布的内阁名单：财政部长，公正党的安华本人兼任；内政部长，公正党的赛夫丁纳苏申；教育部长，公正党的法丽娜。三个位子，全部握在公正党手里。

学费、治安、拨款，这三个部门管的都是华社日常最常翻看的账本，行动党一个都没拿到。行动党拿到的是交通部、房屋及地方政府部，同样重要，只是不是华社过年过节最常念叨的那几本账。

真纳这一席也是同样的算法：陆兆福守了十三年，二〇二三年还稳稳拿下两千多票的多数票，二〇二六年输了。

这一次没有内阁名单可以解释，是选民自己投出来的。败选之后，他向中委会请辞森美兰行动党主席，秘书长的位子留住了，真纳的议席没有留住。同一天，马华在真纳与乐巴同时胜出，两个议席一起翻，终结了这个政党自二〇一三年以来在森州议会零议席的纪录。

如果「有人在朝」和「谁在朝、掌了哪个部、这一届选民还愿不愿意再投他一次」中间隔着这么大一段距离，那「华人要有代表，就投行动党」这句话，可能从头到尾只是一种习惯而已。

换个算法，代表更像是每一届选举都要重新算一次的账，会籍不会自动展期。二〇二二年算的是内阁名单，二〇二六年算的是真纳这一席。两次算下来，答案都不是那句老话想要的那个答案。这两笔账都不难查，内阁名单公开可查，真纳的成绩在选举委员会网站也查得到，谁都能自己核对一遍。

下次再听到「投了这一票，华社就有人在朝办事」，可以先问一句：那个人，掌的是哪个部、守的是哪一席？答案不在感觉里，在内阁名单和选举成绩单里。

Someone's in Government. Whose Representative Is That?

If Chinese Malaysians want representation, you vote DAP. That is what our generation has said for close to twenty years. On 30 July, two days before Negeri Sembilan's state election, the president of Huazong (华总, the Federation of Chinese Associations Malaysia), Datuk Lim Ka Chuan, put it more plainly: Chinese voters needed to turn out, he said, to make sure the community would have "someone in government to get things done" after the polls.

The result declared on 1 August answered that question, and it landed on the person best placed to speak for the line. In Chennah, the seat DAP secretary-general Anthony Loke had held from 2013 to 2026, he lost this time: 5,038 votes against 5,726 for MCA's Siow Kong Choon, a margin of 688. The result was declared by the Election Commission and is on the official results portal. Chennah has 14,422 registered voters, and turnout was 75.71%.

Three years, same man, same seat: the majority went from 2,200 to minus 688.

If "someone in government" is what gets things done, look at what happened inside government. In the general election of November 2022, DAP won forty parliamentary seats, the biggest winner within Pakatan Harapan. Open the cabinet list announced that December: the finance portfolio went to PKR's Anwar himself; home affairs went to PKR's Saifuddin Nasution; education went to PKR's Fadhlina. All three went to PKR.

School fees, policing, allocations: the three portfolios closest to a Chinese family's everyday ledger, and none of them went to DAP. What DAP got was transport, and housing and local government, equally important, just not the ledgers a Chinese household reaches for at Chinese New Year.

Chennah runs on the same arithmetic. Loke held the seat for thirteen years, still won comfortably by over two thousand votes in 2023, and lost in 2026.

No cabinet list explains this one. The voters decided it themselves. After the defeat, he resigned as Negeri Sembilan DAP chairman before the party's central committee; he kept the secretary-general's post, he did not keep Chennah. The same day, MCA won both Chennah and Lobak, two seats flipping at once, ending the party's 13-year run of holding zero seats in the Negeri Sembilan state assembly.

If there is this much distance between "someone in government" and "who exactly is in government, holding which ministry, and whether this round's voters will back him again," then "vote DAP for representation" may, from start to finish, have only ever been a habit.

Do the arithmetic differently, and representation looks less like a membership that renews itself and more like a ledger recalculated at every election. In 2022 the ledger was a cabinet list. In 2026 it was one seat in Chennah. Run it twice, and neither time gives the old line the answer it wanted. Neither record is hard to check: the cabinet list is public, and Chennah's result is on the Election Commission's website too, anyone can check it themselves.

Next time someone says "cast this vote and the Chinese community will have someone in government," it is worth asking first: which ministry does that person hold, which seat did they actually keep? The answer is not a feeling. It is in the cabinet list and the election results.
