财案里的三层楼

Three Floors in the Budget

逆流 · 2026年10月11日

茶桌上算工资，先别只问两千够不够，也看看这张工资单有几层。《星报》报道，2027年财案把最低薪金从每月1700令吉调至2000令吉，2027年6月起生效，涉及超过400万名工人。每月多300令吉，增幅约17.6%。这是一楼，地板。

半熟练工人与大学毕业生的最低月薪定为2500令吉，是再上一层。财案里写着，官联投资公司与官联公司的生活工资从每月3100令吉调至3400令吉，涉及23万名员工。3400令吉比2000令吉高1400令吉。三层数字摆在同一张茶室账单上，楼层不同，工资线也不同。

再看楼下店铺的工资单。《星报》报道，年销售额低于5000万令吉的中小微型企业有一段调整期；马新社报道，这些企业暂获豁免，好让它们调整商业模式。两家都没有说明期限。如果拿最低薪金的工人大多就在这些企业里，那么2027年6月是新薪金的生效日，却未必是这些企业工资单改变的日子。从1700走到2000，对谁来说是六月，对谁来说是“另行通知”？

English version · translated from the Chinese original

Three Floors in the Budget

When working out wages at the tea table, don't just ask whether two thousand is enough; look at how many floors this pay slip has. The Star reported that Budget 2027 raises the minimum wage from RM1,700 to RM2,000 a month, effective June 2027, covering more than 4 million workers. That is RM300 more a month, an increase of about 17.6%. This is the ground floor, the floor itself.

The minimum monthly pay for semi-skilled workers and university graduates is set at RM2,500, one floor up. The Budget states that the living wage for government-linked investment companies and government-linked companies rises from RM3,100 to RM3,400 a month, covering 230,000 employees. RM3,400 is RM1,400 higher than RM2,000. Three sets of figures sit on the same tea-house bill, yet on different floors the wage lines differ.

Now look at the pay slips of the shops downstairs. The Star reported that micro, small and medium enterprises with annual sales below RM50 million get a period of adjustment; Bernama reported that these businesses are temporarily exempt so they can adjust their business models. Neither gives a time limit. If most of the workers on the minimum wage are in these businesses, then June 2027 is the date the new wage takes effect, but not necessarily the day these businesses' pay slips change. From 1,700 to 2,000: for whom is it June, and for whom is it "until further notice"?
