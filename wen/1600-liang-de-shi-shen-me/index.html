1600量的是什么

What the 1,600 Line Measures

逆流 · 2026年10月10日

财案公布那周，马股一跌，茶桌上很容易把两件事连起来：是不是财案拖累股市，经济也不行？星洲日报报道，综指盘中低见1599.21点，写下自2025年9月以来的13个月新低；全周下跌1.37%。

先看银行。星洲日报报道，金融服务指数全周跌3.23%，是跌幅最大的板块。摩根大通下调联昌集团和大马银行评级，报告指长期债券收益率上升会侵蚀银行交易收益、资本水平和派息能力。若导火线是全球债息，便不能把指数下跌全记在财案账上。综指只有三十家成分股，银行权重高；1600点量到的，可能是大银行资产负债表对全球债息的体温。茨厂街和巴刹的温度，未必在其中。

同一周，星洲日报报道，创业板涨2.26%，工业、医疗、物流板块也都上扬，成交量还比上周多。鼎峰证券指出，2000令吉最低薪资给劳动密集行业带来盈利压力，财案的财政纪律有利消费，但家庭措施无法抵销企业层面的影响；外资撤离仍是马股升势的阻力。成本先写进企业损益表，家庭的开销却在饭桌上。指数跌，不等于普通人日子变差；指数守住，也不等于日子守住。

What the 1,600 Line Measures

In the week the Budget was announced, once the Malaysian stock market fell, it was easy at the tea table to link two things: is the Budget dragging down the market, and is the economy not doing well either? Sin Chew Daily reported that the KLCI dipped to 1,599.21 intraday, a 13-month low since September 2025, and fell 1.37% over the week.

Look at the banks first. Sin Chew Daily reported that the financial services index fell 3.23% over the week, the biggest drop among sectors. JPMorgan downgraded CIMB and AmBank, with its report saying that rising long-term bond yields would erode banks' trading gains, capital levels and dividend capacity. If the trigger was global bond yields, the index's fall cannot be put entirely on the Budget's account. The KLCI has only thirty constituents and a heavy bank weighting; what the 1,600 line measured may be the body temperature of the big banks' balance sheets against global bond yields. The temperature of Jalan Petaling and the wet market may not be in it.

In the same week, Sin Chew Daily reported, the ACE Market rose 2.26%, and the industrial, healthcare and logistics sectors also gained, with trading volume higher than the week before. Apex Securities pointed out that the RM2,000 minimum wage puts earnings pressure on labour-intensive industries; the Budget's fiscal discipline favours consumption, but household measures cannot offset the effect at the corporate level, and foreign outflows remain a drag on the market's uptrend. Costs are written first into companies' income statements, while household spending sits on the dinner table. An index falling does not mean ordinary people's lives got worse; an index holding does not mean their lives are holding either.
