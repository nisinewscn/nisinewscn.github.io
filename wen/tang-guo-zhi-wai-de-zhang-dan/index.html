糖果之外的账单

The Bill Beyond the Candy

逆流 · 2026年10月7日

星期五看预算案，先别急着问它甜不甜。安华10月7日在吉隆坡向媒体简报，2027年预算案将在10月9日提呈，是任内第五份财案。《星报》报道，他说，若人民仍承受生活成本上涨压力，单靠经济增长并不足够；预算案将继续聚焦减轻负担，让发展成果惠及人民。

糖果预算案？The Rakyat Post的标题这样问。该报道说，预算案星期五下午3时30分在国会下议院提呈，也是第16届大选前最后一份预算案；BUDI95有1400万人使用，汽油配额自9月1日起恢复每月300公升。

茶室结账时，补贴像替人付了一部分账；可油价之外，那碗面为什么贵，账单未必说得明白。1400万人使用BUDI95，是帮人付账的尺度；它说明多少人需要帮助，却没有说明物价为什么涨。糖果当天尝到，减轻负担则要明年十月还感觉得到。星期五看直播，不妨问：哪项拨款压住价格本身，哪项只是帮人结账？

The Bill Beyond the Candy

When you watch Friday's Budget, don't rush to ask whether it is sweet. At a media briefing in Kuala Lumpur on 7 October, Anwar said Budget 2027 will be tabled on 9 October, the fifth of his term. The Star reported him saying that if people are still bearing rising living costs, economic growth alone is not enough; the Budget will continue to focus on easing the burden, so that the fruits of development reach the people.

A sweetener budget? That is the question The Rakyat Post's headline asks. The report says the Budget will be tabled in the Dewan Rakyat at 3.30pm on Friday, and is the last Budget before the 16th general election; BUDI95 has 14 million users, and the petrol quota returned to 300 litres a month from 1 September.

At the kopitiam counter, a subsidy is like someone paying part of your bill; but beyond the price of fuel, the bill may not explain why that bowl of noodles costs so much. Fourteen million BUDI95 users is the measure of help with paying; it shows how many people need help, but not why prices rose. The candy is tasted that very day, while easing the burden has to still be felt next October. When watching the live broadcast on Friday, it is worth asking: which allocation holds down prices themselves, and which merely helps people settle the bill?
