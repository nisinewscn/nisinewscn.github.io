逆的是惯性

Against Habit, Not Against Anyone

逆流 · 2026年7月29日

有些意见是我们自己想出来的，有些是继承来的。分辨的方法不难：说得出理由和来源的，是想出来的；说不出的，多半是继承的。

对伊党的看法，你属于哪一种？

这个问题，我在茶室里问过不少朋友。得到的答案通常很快，很整齐，几乎一字不差。快，整齐，一字不差，恰恰说明它们来自同一个地方。不是来自各自的观察，是来自同一套从小听到大的说法。

《逆思》想做的事只有一件：把这些说法摊开来，对着可以查证的记录看一遍。

州财政预算是公开的。国会记录是公开的。竞选宣言、内阁名单、审计报告，都是公开的。奇怪的是，华社谈政治谈得这么热闹，真正翻开这些文件的人却这么少。我们宁可转发一段来路不明的文字，也不愿意花五分钟查一个数字。

这里不一样。这里的每一个数字都注明出处，让你自己去核对。

先把话说清楚。这个网站不骂人。不揣测任何政治人物的动机，无论他来自哪个政党。不告诉你该投谁。做的只是算账：把承诺和结果摆在一起，把拨款和人口摆在一起，把三十年的记录和三十秒的口号摆在一起。

名字里的「逆」，逆的是惯性。

一个想法在脑子里住了三十年，不代表它是对的，只代表它住得够久，久到我们忘了问它是怎么进来的。逆思，就是把它请出来，验一验证件。

每星期两三篇。中文写，附英文版。写的人是谁不重要：一个马来西亚华人，一个政治观察者。重要的是文中引用的文件，你随时可以自己打开。

如果哪一篇看完，你查了出处，想法还是原来的想法，那很好。

至少这一次，它是你自己的。

Against Habit, Not Against Anyone

Some of our opinions we worked out ourselves. Others we inherited. Telling them apart is not hard: if you can name the reason and the source, you worked it out; if you cannot, you probably inherited it.

Which kind is your opinion of PAS?

I have put that question to more than a few friends across kopitiam tables. The answers usually come fast, uniform, almost word for word. Fast, uniform, word for word: which is exactly what tells you they come from the same place. Not from each person's own observation, but from the same script we all grew up hearing.

《逆思》 (NISI) wants to do one thing only: lay those scripts out and read them against records that can be checked.

State budgets are public. Parliamentary records are public. Election manifestos, cabinet lists, audit reports: all public. The odd thing is that for a community that talks politics as loudly as ours does, so few of us ever open these documents. We would rather forward a paragraph of unknown origin than spend five minutes checking one number.

This site is different. Every figure here carries its source, so you can check it yourself.

Let us be clear from the start. This site does not attack people. It does not speculate about any politician's motives, whatever their party. It does not tell you how to vote. All it does is arithmetic: promises set next to outcomes, allocations set next to populations, thirty years of records set next to thirty seconds of slogans.

The 逆 (against) in our name pushes against habit, nothing else.

An opinion that has lived in your head for thirty years is not therefore right. It has simply stayed long enough that we forgot to ask how it got in. To think against the current is to invite that opinion out and check its papers.

Two or three pieces a week. Written in Chinese, with an English version attached. Who writes them matters little: a Chinese Malaysian, a political observer. What matters are the documents cited, which you can open for yourself at any time.

If you finish a piece, check the sources, and your opinion stays exactly where it was, good.

At least this time, it is your own.
